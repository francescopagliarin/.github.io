01 About

About me

My research lies at the intersection of analysis and nonlinear partial differential equations. I am interested in understanding qualitative properties of solutions, sharp functional inequalities, and the regularity of elliptic equations.

This page is a work in progress. Further details about my academic activities will be added here.
